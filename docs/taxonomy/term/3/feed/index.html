number of pages that are analyzed.</li><li>﻿﻿Very difficult to iterate or change course (such as if you decide to change the definition of audience)</li><li>﻿﻿Very difficult to merge analysis from multiple people.</li><li>﻿﻿Very difficult to be consistent.</li><li>﻿﻿Difficult to track over time.</li><li>﻿﻿Likely to encourage team to take action on content while analyzing it.</li></ul><h2>Disadvantages of one-off Al</h2><ul><li>﻿﻿Generally low transparency (How did it come up with the answer? Did it look at any pages? Did it consider your existing analysis?)</li><li>﻿﻿Related to the above, it can definitely hallucinate or over-extrapolate.</li><li>﻿﻿Generally requires powerful LLM</li><li>﻿﻿Can short circuit critical thinking of the analyst</li><li>﻿﻿NOT for pervasive analysis across a digital presence</li></ul><p>Tip: If you do one-off chatbot analysis, explicitly ask how it came up with the answer and its sources. ie., "Explain to me in detail how you determined this.
