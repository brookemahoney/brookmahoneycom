The lost in the middle problem becomes much less of a problem."</li><li data-list-item-id="eb4428b2d5c2b287f6b641539beebf622">29:34 "if you compartmentalize nicely works pretty well just like a human."</li></ul></li><li data-list-item-id="e13d2fef9e8c4c638c783763ab615c3b6">Bob uses an agile mindset when working with agents (instead of a waterfall-like approach with very rigid specs and planning beforehand).<ul><li data-list-item-id="eaa40844dca35ca3f27ea0d976a725612">36:49 "Wait a minute.
