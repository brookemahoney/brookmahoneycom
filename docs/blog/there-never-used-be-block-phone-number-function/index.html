There Never Used To Be A Block Phone Number Function

When I was young, there weren't even answering machines. I was born in 1977. Anyway, I just thought of "when I pick up the phone, there's still nobody home" from Pink Floyd's The Wall. Nowadays, calling someone is usually not calling their home. If they don't answer, maybe they saw it was you and didn't want to talk. Maybe you are on an auto-voicemail list. Or, you know. That's all normal now.

But before, we didn't really have that. We all went about our day without going home to talk on the phone or check if we got mail that day. We did things all day and then came back to check in. Maybe on the weekends, the phone would ring more in the middle of the day. Someone almost always answered it.

You just go to the phone, and you say, "hello?". It wasn't very complicated. Thing felt safer then.
