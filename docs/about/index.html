About

Hi, I'm Brooke :) Nice to meet you or maybe I already know you?

I am just a human being. I suppose my favorite things are people, animals, wildlife, science, philosophy, and music. I've always been curious about the nature of things in general.

I was born in 1977 in Wisconsin and moved to California in 1988, where I live today.

My current life

Lately I've been fascinated with the movie Wisdom of Happiness and spend a lot of time meditating.

I also have been working on brushing up my Drupal related skills and planning to get back in to the workforce full-time.

And I love hanging out with my cat Luna.

My past

Born in January 1977. Grew up in a small town till I was 10. Parents moved us to California for jobs. I always did well in school but lacked direction until later on in life.

After high school, I went to Europe for a couple of months. Then to UCSB in 1995, where I was supposed to learn microbiology and get a general education but instead I became fascinated with the internet and learned how to code websites. I've been making websites either full-time or part-time since then, even when working other jobs.

But I couldn't decide on a direction to take and made a wild decision to go to music school in Hollywood at the Musicians Instititute of Technology. I learned audio mastering and mixing there, though I was supposed to be learning to be a better guitar player. After that I did community college in Riverside, CA, and moved in with my girlfriend at the time, who is amazing still (you know who you are) and across town from my troublemaker of a brother. Then we broke up, it was not easy, and I moved to Santa Rosa with my brother and his friend. But, they didn't like having jobs or paying rent, so I moved out on my own.

I worked a ton of odds jobs, played some music, and moved around the town of Santa Rosa until 2011, when I met my ex-wife on a radio show. That's when I start doing computer programming full time and specializing in Drupal for work. I spent a decade making complex websites for some of the biggest companies in the world. But, it was a very difficult decade, ending with COVID and resulting in our marriage starting to waver. I got sick with COVID and had some knocks to the head dirt biking and from a faint episode I had from a brain malformation I have. Good times. I think I became quite horrible to live with and quite negative. So, she left in 2023. May she be peaceful and happy.

It's three years later, more or less, and I'm fairly healed and ready to go again. That's the plan anyway. I've learned a lot in these three years. Maybe I'll blog about some of it here. I don't know.
