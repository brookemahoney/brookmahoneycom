About

Hi, I'm Brooke :) Nice to meet you or maybe I already know you?

My favorites in life: people, animals, wildlife, science, philosophy, and music. I've always been curious about the nature of things in general.

I was born in 1977 in Wisconsin and moved to California in 1987, where I live today.

My current life

Lately I've been fascinated with the movie Wisdom of Happiness and spend a lot of time meditating.

I work a lot with Drupal.

And I love hanging out with my cat, Luna.

My past

I was born in January 1977 and grew up in a small town of about 4000 people till I was ten years old. My parents moved us to California for jobs.

After high school, I went to Europe for a couple of months. Then to UCSB in 1995, where I was supposed to learn microbiology and get a general education but instead I became fascinated with the internet and learned how to code websites. I've been making websites either full-time or part-time since then, even when working other jobs.

But I couldn't decide on a direction to take at the university. I made a wild decision to go to music school in Hollywood at the Musicians Instititute of Technology where I first learned about digital audio mastering and mixing. After that I did community college in Riverside, CA and eventually moved to Santa Rosa, a quieter town just an hour's drive North of San Francisco.

I worked a ton of odds jobs, played some music, and hung out at the college coffee shop a lot. I did a lot of web work for local businesses. Then in 2011, I met my ex-wife on a radio show and later that year moved to her town where we stayed together for about 12 years. That's when I start doing computer programming full time and specializing in Drupal for work. I spent over a decade making complex websites for some of the biggest companies in the world.

But COVID happened and I got it really bad with pneumonia and bronchitis in March of 2020. I almost died, I'd say. It took a couple of years to really recover. Work was very difficult. I became quite exhausted, as did a lot of people. Then, in the span of a year in 2023, my cat died and my wife left me, despite me trying very hard to keep the marriage going. I was devastated. I took time off from full-time work to focus on personal healing.

I rescued a new cat, Luna <3. I worked at the animal shelter. I travelled to Europe to see a friend who was feeling down. I read a ton of books. I wrote so much in personal journals. I meditated. Now I've had a few of years to really think about life and what I want to do with the rest of mine. I'm turning 50 this year.

I'm ready to go again, stronger than ever. I'm excited about the future. I'm hopeful and determined. When I work nowadays, I do it happily. Maybe I'll blog about some of it here. I don't know.
