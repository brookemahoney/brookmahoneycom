Major Depressive Disorder, Recurrent (MDD)

This means I have times in my life, over and over throughout it, that I do not wish to live. When I was 18, I tried to kill myself. A friend found me in the act. They saved my life. I thank that person a lot, just not IRL, because we don't talk anymore. Most people don't talk to me anymore. They don't like the depression.

Nowadays, I still don't have the will to live. But, I made a committment to my cat. He always loves me. He doesn't care if I'm depressed, he just wants my company. We feel like we can do life when we're together. It would be nice if people could hang out with me, but they can't, it seems. So, when my cat dies, I'll get another cat, and so on.
