"When we make a habit of compassion in our everyday lives through regular practice and action, we live with more courage, less stress, and greater freedom.

In time, we will automatically see ourselves, and the world, in terms of interconnectedness.

Our default position towards other people will be as fellow human beings, rather than sources of antagonism and threat.

Wisdom Of Happiness

About This Page

This is a transcript of Wisdom of Happiness, which is narrated by His Holiness The Dalai Lama at age 89. He has spent his life thinking about the wellbeing and suffering of all sentient beings. In this film he has distilled down some of the most important concepts for peace of mind and a more peaceful society. It really touched me; so much that I wrote down every word. I did not copy this transcript, I paused and rewound, and listened as carefully as I could.

Brothers and sisters of this small planet
some of you may come here just out of curiosity
then people who come here with some expectation
some kind of miracle blessing or something like that
then don't expect much

I want to make clear
we are same human beings
now, if you give me permission to use my broken English

Deep inside, without any question,
everybody, every sentient being,
wants peace, happiness
